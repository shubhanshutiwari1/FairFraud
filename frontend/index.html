About the Pipeline

Built on the Bank Account Fraud benchmark dataset with sentinel-aware preprocessing, multi-family inference, and fairness verification.

01 / PREPROCESSING

Sentinel-Aware Feature Engineering

While raw columns have 0% null strings, negative sentinel codes (-1 in address months and negative balance transfers) are dynamically converted to NaN, imputed, scaled, and one-hot encoded into a 55-dimensional tensor.

02 / 7-MODEL ENSEMBLE

Cross-Family Consensus

Evaluates CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost, TabNet, FT-Transformer, SAINT, and GraphSAGE simultaneously—comparing tree, attention-tabular, and graph predictions against a 50.0% decision boundary.

03 / XAI & FAIRNESS

Explainability & Age Parity

Measures local marginal risk contributions for each numerical input and runs counterfactual age-bracket audits (<50 vs ≥50) to ensure predictions remain fair across applicant demographics.
